January 30, 2025

VAM Symposium 2025: VAM in Action

Bridging academic research and real-world solutions

The 2025 symposium explored emerging end uses of VAM technologies across industrial sectors. It ran as a single track from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM in the Sequoia Room at the Helen Diller Anchor House on the UC Berkeley campus, primarily in person with a virtual option, and was scheduled alongside SPIE Photonics West in San Francisco.

The program comprised two plenary talks and three moderated panels, with a catered lunch for posters, lab tours, and demonstrations. This format continues in 2027.
