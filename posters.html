Call for posters · Now open

Submit a poster

The poster session is the primary venue for students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career scientists to present their work. Abstracts are reviewed as they arrive; the deadline is Tuesday, December 8, 2026. Submit your abstract below.

How to submit

Posters are displayed throughout the Friday symposium in The Gateway, with an attended session during the catered lunch. Work in progress, hardware builds, software tools, and materials research are all welcome, as are negative results.

Poster topics

Submissions are welcome under any of the symposium topic areas:

  1. Light engines and holography
  2. Projection computation and inverse design
  3. Photochemistry and resin design
  4. Process monitoring, metrology, and control
  5. Scale, throughput, and production
  6. Materials beyond acrylates
  7. Biofabrication
  8. Open hardware, reproducibility, and education

Submission guidelines

  • An abstract of up to 250 words, with a title, the presenting author, and any co-authors.
  • Unpublished and in-progress work is welcome. Only the title and author list appear in the program; abstracts are not published.
  • Posters in portrait orientation, up to 36 × 48 inches (A0 also fits). Boards and pins are provided.
  • The presenting author attends the Friday symposium in person. Registration is free.

Timeline

  • Now: submissions open and are reviewed on a rolling basis.
  • Tuesday, December 8, 2026: abstract deadline.
  • Tuesday, December 15, 2026: all decisions sent.

Live demonstrations

Bench space and power can usually be provided next to the poster boards for hardware demonstrations. Tick the live demonstration option on the submission form if you plan to bring equipment.
